## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes mandatory operational rules for using MedDefense Health Systems computing assets, network infrastructure, and electronic data. It exists to safeguard Protected Health Information (PHI), maintain operational availability, and protect MedDefense from security risks and legal liability.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies directly to every individual who has been granted access to any MedDefense system, network, workstation, or data repository. Covered individuals specifically include:

- Full-time and part-time MedDefense employees
- Independent contractors and temporary agency personnel
- Third-party vendor representatives with system access
- Temporary clinical staff, including traveling nurses, locum tenens physicians, and clinical residents

## 3. Acceptable Use

MedDefense systems exist for authorized patient care and business only. Personal use is strictly prohibited.

Users are expected to utilize company resources responsibly, ethically, and in direct alignment with their assigned professional responsibilities. Any non-work-related activity on company assets is strictly prohibited.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on all MedDefense systems, devices, and networks. No exceptions are permitted without explicit, written authorization from IT Security:

- **Patient Records:** Accessing, viewing, or querying patient records outside an active care relationship or authorized job duty for that specific patient. Looking up records of family, friends, colleagues, or public figures without clinical justification is prohibited.
- **Credentials:** Sharing passwords, PINs, or multi-factor authentication tokens. Permitting another person—including colleagues or supervisors—to operate under your logged-in account is strictly prohibited. Each person must use their own assigned account.
- **Software and Devices:** Downloading or installing unauthorized software, scripts, or browser extensions. Connecting unauthorized personal USB/external drives or personal devices to the clinical network without IT enrollment and approval.
- **Data Handling:** Transmitting patient data via personal email, personal cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox), unapproved messaging tools (e.g., WhatsApp, iMessage), or non-BAA external services. Pasting patient data or internal documentation into external AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) or exporting data to personal devices is strictly prohibited.
- **Access Boundaries:** Attempting to access systems, data, or network segments beyond your assigned role. Performing administrative actions without explicit IT authorization.
- **Misuse & Disruption:** Using company resources for personal business, illegal content, harassment, circumventing security controls, or taking actions that disrupt clinical care.

## 5. Remote Access Requirements

If you work remotely or away from a clinical facility, you must adhere to the following operational requirements:

- **VPN Mandatory:** Always connect through the official MedDefense VPN when working remotely. Accessing systems over public Wi-Fi without an active VPN is strictly prohibited.
- **Device Enrolment & Encryption:** Work only on authorized devices enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM) with full disk encryption enabled.
- **Screen Privacy:** Work in a private area so screen contents are not visible to family members, visitors, or the public. Always lock your screen (Windows Key + L) or log out when stepping away.

## 6. Reporting Obligations

Report immediately upon discovery (do not wait until the end of your shift):

- Suspicious emails, phishing attempts, or unexpected password reset requests.
- Lost, stolen, or misplaced devices (laptops, badges, phones, encrypted drives).
- Anomalous system behavior, unexpected pop-ups, or malware signs.
- Accidental disclosure of PHI or confidential data.
- Security policy violations observed by others.

**How to Report:**

- **IT Security Ticket:** Submit via https://helpdesk.meddefense.internal (Mark as Security Incident).
- **Urgent Phone Line:** Call IT Security at Ext. 4357 (HELP) / (555) 019-4357.
- **Confidential Escalation:** Email ciso@meddefense.health.

## 7. Consequences of Policy Violation

Violations of this policy will result in immediate investigation and severe disciplinary actions:

- Immediate access revocation
- HR disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract cancellation
- Loss of clinical privileges
- Referral to law enforcement and OCR reporting where legally mandated
- **Personal HIPAA Liability:** Violations involving unauthorized access, theft, or misuse of PHI carry statutory civil penalties starting at $100 per violation per day, as well as potential federal criminal charges. Personal legal liability applies independently, regardless of internal MedDefense disciplinary action.

## 8. Acknowledgment and Review

All workforce members must sign this policy during onboarding before system access is granted. Mandatory re-acknowledgment is required annually during compliance training.
