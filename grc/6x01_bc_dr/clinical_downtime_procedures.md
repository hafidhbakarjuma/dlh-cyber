# MedDefense Clinical Downtime Procedures Quick-Reference

**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Operational / Clinical Staff Reference  
**Distribution:** All Nursing Stations, Emergency Department, Intensive Care Unit, and Laboratory Reception  

---

## 1. Epic EHR Downtime Quick Reference

When the Electronic Health Record (Epic EHR) becomes unavailable, clinical care must continue without interruption using standardized paper-based documentation workflows. **Patient safety, accurate medication administration, and legible documentation are your primary priorities.**

### Declaration and Immediate Actions
1. **Declare Downtime:** The Charge Nurse or Nursing Supervisor formally declares Epic EHR downtime upon confirming system loss exceeding 15 minutes or receiving official notification from IT.
2. **Access Physical Binders:** Immediately retrieve the **Downtime Documentation Binder** from the designated secure wall cabinet at your nursing station (Locations: ICU Station Alpha, ED Triage Desk, 3rd Floor East Nurses' Station).
3. **Initiate Paper Forms:** Distribute **Form DT-101 (Inpatient Downtime Charting Sheet)** and **Form DT-202 (Manual Medication Administration Record - MAR)** to all attending nurses for active patient assignments.

### Clinical Workflow Execution
4. **Manual Vitals and Assessments:** Record all vital signs, neurological checks, and clinical assessments manually on Form DT-101 using **black or dark blue ink**. Include exact times and initials for every entry.
5. **Double-Check Medications:** **Perform mandatory two-nurse independent double-checks for all high-alert medications and insulin administration.** Record batch numbers and expiration dates manually on Form DT-202 before administration.
6. **Physician Orders:** Receive verbal or handwritten telephone orders using the strict **Read-Back-Verify** protocol. Have a second licensed nurse co-sign all telephone orders on the paper order sheet.

### Escalation Path
*   **Level 1 (0–30 minutes):** Notify Nursing Supervisor and Unit Charge Nurse. Log outage start time.
*   **Level 2 (30+ minutes):** Contact the IT Help Desk Command Post at extension 4400 to verify outage ticket logging.
*   **Level 3 (Severe Clinical Impact):** Contact the Chief Medical Officer (Dr. Morales) via the hospital operator for acute resource allocation.

### Post-Restoration Reconciliation Steps
1. **Do Not Destroy Paper Records:** All downtime paper forms remain legal medical records and must be retained in the patient chart folder until data entry is complete.
2. **Back-Entry Verification:** Once IT confirms Epic EHR restoration, designated clinical data entry scribes will enter all paper-charted data back into Epic within 12 hours of system recovery.
3. **Audit and Sign-Off:** The attending nurse must review the back-entered electronic record against the paper downtime forms, sign the reconciliation completion log, and file paper forms in the permanent medical records archive.

## 2. Laboratory Information System (LIS) Downtime Quick Reference

When the Laboratory Information System (LIS) is offline, diagnostic specimen ordering, specimen labeling, and result reporting transition to manual paper and courier protocols. **Never delay STAT life-saving blood draws due to computer downtime.**

### Declaration and Immediate Actions
1. **Declare Downtime:** The Lead Laboratory Technologist or ED Charge Nurse declares LIS downtime upon confirmation of system failure.
2. **Retrieve LIS Downtime Pack:** Obtain **Form DT-303 (Manual Lab Requisition & Result Log)** and pre-printed barcode patient identification stickers from the central lab reception drawer.
3. **Manual Requisitioning:** Fill out Form DT-303 completely for all pending lab tests, including patient full name, MRN, date of birth, ordering physician, and clinical priority (STAT vs. Routine).

### Specimen Collection and Transport Execution
4. **Mandatory Patient Verification:** **Verify patient identity using two unique identifiers (Full Name and MRN) against the patient identification band before drawing any specimen.**
5. **Manual Tube Labeling:** Handwrite the patient MRN, full name, date, time of collection, and phlebotomist initials directly on every specimen tube label if barcode label printers are offline.
6. **Physical Courier Transport:** Transport STAT specimens (blood gases, cardiac markers, type-and-cross) immediately via physical courier runner to the core laboratory receiving window. Do not use pneumatic tube systems if system validation is unverified.

### Result Reporting and Escalation
7. **Manual Result Notification:** Laboratory staff will perform manual bench testing, record results on physical worksheets, and phone critical results directly to the requesting unit charge nurse using mandatory read-back verification.
8. **Escalation Path:** For delayed critical lab results exceeding 45 minutes from collection, contact the Laboratory Manager on call via the hospital operator or reference **Clinical Escalation Contact List (Ext. 3200)**.

### Post-Restoration Reconciliation Steps
9. **Result Batch Entry:** Laboratory technicians will enter all manual test results into the restored LIS database, cross-referencing specimen accession numbers against Form DT-303 logs.
10. **Chart Matching:** Nurses must verify that electronic lab results in Epic/LIS match the telephoned and paper-recorded critical values in the patient downtime binder. Sign off on final result reconciliation within 8 hours of LIS restoration.
